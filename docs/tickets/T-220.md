Integrate `egui_dock` crate and add `DockState` to `AppState` for the workbench docking framework.

- [ ] No compilation errors; CI passes

- `apps/lintel/src/settings.rs` (AppSettings)
- `apps/lintel/src/panels.rs` (new: panel enum)

- Panel content
